> **Source:** PeptiStrong® (Nuritas) — full claim list per `Nuritas - PeptiStrong US Claim list_Jan 25.pdf` (US market, Jan 2025).
>
> **Trademark:** PeptiStrong® is a Nuritas Limited trademark. First mention in any document carries `®`; subsequent mentions in the same surface drop the symbol.
>
> **PeptiStrong® is a Vicia faba (fava bean) peptide network — a plant-based ingredient.** This is critical positioning leverage: every "milk protein" comparator below is *also* a plant-vs-dairy story. Boss Mode Myo-Peptide should own this.

## What changed with the full claim list

The supplier package contains **three published RCTs in humans** plus mechanism + preclinical data. Human RCT claims are categorically stronger than the mechanism claims and should now lead.

| Study | Dose | Population | Endpoint |
|---|---|---|---|
| **RCT-1 (ref 3)** | 2.4g/day | Healthy males | Strength recovery, fatigue |
| **RCT-2 (ref 4)** | 2.4g/day | Resistance-trained | Strength + endurance gains |
| **RCT-3 (ref 5)** | 20g/day | Immobilization model | Muscle mass + strength recovery |

> **Boss Mode dose check:** the can advertises **2.4g PeptiStrong®** — this matches RCT-1 and RCT-2. Claims from those two studies are dose-supported. **Claims from RCT-3 are at 20g/day** — do NOT use those without a footnote disclosing the dose mismatch, or pull them entirely. (See "Demote" below.)

## TIER 1 — Hero claims (above-fold, paid ads, can secondary panel)

These are quantified, human-RCT-backed, dose-matched to the 2.4g serving.

- **Increases muscle recovery rate by 144%.**³
- **Reduces muscular fatigue by 47%.**³
- **A 54% increase in performance during recovery vs. placebo.**³

### Strength + endurance (RCT-2, ref 4, 2.4g/day)
- **Increases strength by up to 17%.**⁴
- **Improves muscular endurance up to 21.6%.**⁴
- **Increases muscle energy by up to 49%.**⁴

### Recommended primary lockup
> **Recover 144% faster.**³
> Clinically proven plant-based peptides. 2.4g per can.

> **Why these:** all are quantified, all are from human RCTs at the 2.4g dose, all translate to gym-floor language without softening.

## TIER 3 — Supporting claims (long-form `/science`, FAQ, email nurture)

Mechanism + biomarker claims for readers who want the science.

**Mechanism:**
- Significantly increases phosphorylation of S6 (mTOR / mTORC1 activation).¹·²
- Produces 56% myostatin inhibition.³
- Reduces muscle protein breakdown via Atrogin-1 and MURF1.¹
- Significantly reduces TNF-α, a marker of exercise-induced inflammation.¹

**Energy + glucose:**
- Affects irisin, a plasma marker that promotes glucose homeostasis.³
- Regulates serum markers (FSTL-1, Fractalkine, Apelin, FABP3) related to glucose homeostasis.⁴

**Recovery biology:**
- Affects fractalkine — a factor that promotes muscle repair after exercise.³
- Several myokines significantly increased directly after exercise.³
- Affects serum markers related to muscle regeneration/synthesis.³

**Bone:**
- Regulates serum markers (Oncostatin, Osteocrin) related to bone and muscle maintenance/regeneration.⁴

## TIER 4 — Demote / handle with care

### RCT-3 (immobilization, 20g/day) — DO NOT use raw at 2.4g serving
The following claims come from a 20g/day dose. Boss Mode delivers 2.4g. **Either pull these entirely or rewrite with an explicit dose footnote.** Recommended: pull from PDP, allow only on `/science` long-form with dose disclosure.

- ~~Produces a 103% in leg strength recovery vs only 68% for milk protein.⁵~~ *(at 20g/day)*
- ~~4x more effective than milk protein in improving lean muscle protein synthesis.⁵~~ *(at 20g/day)*
- ~~90% of PeptiStrong participants exhibited an anabolic response compared to 60% with milk protein.⁵~~ *(at 20g/day)*

These are the most marketable claims in the entire package — but they are not earned by the current can dose. **Recommendation:** explore a higher-dose SKU (a "Recovery" or "Pro" line at 10–20g) that earns these claims directly.

### Hedge-required
- "May support age-related muscle weakness."¹ — keep "may," use only in 50+ targeted material.
- "May support building muscle mass."⁵ — same handling.

### In-vitro / preclinical only — technical surfaces only
- "In vitro shown to increase mitochondrial mass.\*"
- "Increases protein synthesis greater than whey.\*" — flagged in Nuritas list as in-vitro unpublished. **Do NOT use in hero copy.** Move to demote.
- "Reduces lean muscle loss by up to 54.3% more effectively than milk protein."¹ — preclinical (rodent hindlimb unloading). Strong-sounding but not human. Demote from Tier 1 (where I had it before) to Tier 3 with `†` footnote.
- "60% reduction in muscle atrophy-induced inflammation."† — same handling.

## Open / strategic asks

1. **The 20g claims are the most powerful in the entire package.** Strongly recommend exploring a "Boss Mode Myo-Peptide PRO" or "Recovery Stack" SKU at 10–20g/serving to legitimately use them. Worth a separate strategy session.
2. **RCT-2 (ref 4) is "manuscript submitted."** Confirm publication status before launch — claims sourced from unpublished manuscripts can carry regulatory and PR risk.
3. **Plant-based positioning is underplayed.** Vicia faba peptides + clinical efficacy at the same rate as dairy = a category lever competitors can't touch. Consider elevating to brand-level positioning.
4. **EU/CA market expansion.** EU has banned several of these claim phrasings outright (anything around "muscle protein synthesis" + recovery numbers needs EFSA backing). Flag if needed.
