## VOICE GUARDRAILS

- **Always use the trademarks:** Boss Mode™, Myo-Peptide™, PeptiStrong®, Sip. Dominate. Repeat.™
- **Numbers do the talking.** Lead with stats when you can. 144%, 47%, 2.4g.
- **Short sentences. Athletic rhythm.** Three-word lines beat ten-word lines.
- **Never apologize, never over-explain.** Confidence is the brand.
- **Avoid:** "we're so excited," "amazing," "game-changer," "🚀✨💪" emoji walls. Pick 0–1 emoji per post, max.
